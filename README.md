## ✨ Features
- Qaza prayer tracker: Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and Witr
- Qaza fasts tracker
- Today's Log: record how many you prayed today
- Progress view: remaining and completed at a glance
- Prayer timings and weekly Jamat times (Bahawalpur)
- Ayah of the Moment with Arabic, Urdu and English translation
- Light, Dark and Night themes with 10 colour choices
- Celebration popup when you complete a target

## 📥 Download and install (Android only)
1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/qaza-umri-app/qaza-umri/releases/latest).
2. Download the `.apk` file from **Assets**.
3. If your phone asks, allow **Install unknown apps**.
4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap **Install anyway**.
5. Open the app and start tracking.

**Updating:** download the new APK and install it over the old one. Do not uninstall first, so your data stays safe.

## 🔒 Privacy
All your qaza data is stored only on your phone. The app uses the internet only to load ayat from the full Quran. Offline, it shows ayat from a built-in collection.
